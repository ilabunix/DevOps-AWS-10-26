UAT Environment and Deployment Readiness

There is an opportunity to strengthen environment management practices by establishing clearer guidelines for development, testing, and release validation. Maintaining UAT as a stable and controlled environment would help ensure consistent testing outcomes and improve overall release readiness.

Additionally, we encountered recurring challenges with application deployments, scripts, and environment readiness that required additional troubleshooting and coordination. Strengthening development-level validation and pre-deployment checks would help identify and address potential issues earlier, reduce rework, and support smoother deployments across environments.
